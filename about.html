Robert J. Riganati is a writer and artist drawn to creating work from lived experience, curiosity, and exploration.

The Present: A Backpacking Adventure in Europe grew from a ten-week solo journey through Europe in 1997 and the desire to preserve the people, places, and moments that made the trip unforgettable.

His visual art comes from a similar impulse: start with an idea, follow it, experiment, and see where it leads. Whether through words or images, the goal is simple—to make something personal, interesting, and worth sharing.
